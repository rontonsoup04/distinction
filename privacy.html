Privacy policy

Distinction (hdistinction.live) is a free, public Q&A forum where university students ask and answer questions about their courses. This policy explains what we collect, why, and the choices you have. We handle personal information in line with the Australian Privacy Principles.

What we collect

  • Account details: your email address and password (stored securely by our authentication provider), or your name and email from Google if you sign in with Google.
  • Profile: your name, display name, university, degree, year of study and preferred language.
  • Posts: the questions and answers you write, your upvotes, and reports you make.

How we use it

  • To run your account and show your posts on the forum.
  • To translate the site, and posts written in another language, into your preferred language.
  • To keep the site safe, handle reports and remove content that breaks our rules, such as questions from open assessments.

We don't sell your information and we don't use it for advertising.

Who can see what

  • Posts are public. Anyone, including people without an account and search engines, can read questions and answers, along with the author's display name and university.
  • Your email address, full name, degree and year of study are not shown on your posts.
  • Who upvoted or reported a post is not shown to other users. Admins can see reports.

Services we use

  • Supabase stores our database and files and handles sign-in.
  • Render hosts the website.
  • Anthropic (Claude) translates text. It receives only the text being translated.
  • Google provides the optional "Continue with Google" sign-in. We receive only your name, email and profile picture.
  • Google Analytics counts visits and which pages and features are used, using cookies and your IP address. We don't send it your name, email or anything you write, and we only use it to improve the site. You can opt out with Google's browser add-on or by blocking cookies.

These providers may store data outside Australia. We only share what each one needs to do its job.

Google sign-in

If you sign in with Google, we request only basic profile information (name, email address and profile picture) to create and identify your account. We don't access your Gmail, Drive, contacts or any other Google data, and we don't share Google user data with anyone except as needed to run your account.

Keeping it and deleting it

We keep your information while your account is active. You can edit your profile, and edit or delete your posts, at any time. To delete your account and the information linked to it, email us and we'll remove it within 30 days, except records we must keep by law.

Security

Data is sent over HTTPS, and database access is restricted with row-level security, so each person can only reach the information they're allowed to see.

Contact

Questions, access or correction requests, or complaints: support@hdistinction.live.

See also our terms of service.
