Who can use Distinction

  • Anyone can read the forum. You need a free account to post, answer, upvote or report.
  • If you're under 18, you need a parent or guardian's permission to create an account.
  • Keep your login details private. You're responsible for what happens on your account.

It's free

Distinction doesn't charge for questions and doesn't pay anyone for answers. Everyone who answers does so voluntarily.

Academic integrity

Distinction is for learning: understanding concepts, course content and how to approach a subject. It is not a place to get assessable work done.

  • Don't post questions from an assignment, quiz or exam that's still open, and don't ask for or post full solutions to assessment tasks.
  • Don't share assessment papers, marking guides or other course materials you don't have the right to share.
  • Follow your university's academic integrity rules. Australian law prohibits providing or advertising academic cheating services.

Posts are public. We remove content that breaks these rules, can suspend accounts, and may cooperate with universities where required by law.

Posting

  • Be respectful. No harassment, hate, spam or advertising.
  • Don't post anyone's personal details, including your own student number.
  • Your display name and university are shown next to your posts.

Your content

You keep ownership of what you post. You give Distinction permission to store it, show it publicly on the site, and translate it into other languages. You can edit or delete your own posts at any time. Don't post anything you don't have the right to share, or anything unlawful, abusive or harassing.

No guarantee on answers

Answers come from other students and may be wrong or incomplete. Use your own judgement, and check anything important against your course materials or teaching staff.

Changes and ending your account
